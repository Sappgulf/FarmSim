# FarmSim Game Guide

## Objective
Grow a thriving farm by planting crops, harvesting for profit, and investing in upgrades, buildings, and automation. Progress through levels to unlock new systems, optimize your farm, and complete daily and weekly contracts for rewards.

## Core Loop
1. **Plant crops** in empty plots.
2. **Water and care** for crops while they grow.
3. **Harvest** ready crops for coins and XP.
4. **Reinvest** in upgrades, buildings, research, animals, and automation.
5. **Complete contracts** to earn bonus rewards and maintain your streak.

## Controls
- **Click/Tap** plots to plant, water, or harvest.
- **Tabs**: Use the sidebar or bottom nav to switch systems.
- **Settings shortcuts**:
  - **Ctrl + S**: Manual save
  - **Space**: Pause/Resume (when not typing)

## Farming Basics
- **Growth**: Crops grow over time and are affected by weather and seasons.
- **Water**: Crops wither if they stay dry. Water regularly.
- **Harvest**: Harvest within the window to avoid withering.
- **Soil Fertility**: Repeated harvesting reduces fertility (fertilize to recover).

## Seasons (Cozy Cycle)
- **Four seasons** rotate on a gentle day-based cadence.
- Each season adds small bonuses to growth and prices.
- **Season day** is shown in the header so you can plan harvest timing.

## Contracts & Quests
- **Daily Quests**: Refresh every midnight. Completing all daily quests increases your streak, boosting rewards.
- **Weekly Contracts**: Refresh every Monday. Larger goals and bigger rewards.
- **Claim Rewards**: Completed objectives must be claimed in the Quests tab.

## Weather (Cozy Forecast)
- Weather stays positive or neutral: **Clear**, **Rain**, **Cloudy**, **Heatwave**.
- Rain helps with watering, cloudy days are gentle, and heatwaves only add mild thirst.
- Forecasts update throughout the day and appear in the Weather tab.

## Town Reputation
- **Town Rep** increases from harvesting and completing contracts.
- Higher rep unlocks small market perks and town flavor bonuses.
- Track your tier and next reward in the Social (Town) tab.

## Collections / Encyclopedia
- The **Collections** tab tracks every crop you discover.
- Milestones: **First Harvest**, **50 Harvests**, **200 Harvests** per crop.
- Use it as a cozy completion checklist and lore book.

## Upgrades & Progression
- **Shop Upgrades**: Permanent tools like watering cans and sprinklers.
- **Research**: Unlock tech upgrades and efficiency boosts.
- **Buildings**: Provide production, storage, and utility benefits.

## Automation (Earned)
- **Sprinkler Tool**: Auto-waters thirsty plots over time once purchased.
- **Well Upgrades**: Higher-level wells provide automatic irrigation.
- **Goal**: Automation reduces micromanagement but is earned through progress.

## Livestock & Fishing
- **Livestock**: Purchase animals, keep them fed and happy, collect products.
- **Fishing**: Play a timing mini-game to catch fish for extra income.

## Saving
- **Auto-save**: Runs every ~30 seconds (toggle in Settings).
- **Manual save**: Use the Save button in the header or Ctrl+S.
- **Safety**: The game keeps a backup save to protect progress.

## Tips
- Chain fast crops early to build a coin buffer.
- Time harvests with seasonal bonuses.
- Use quests/contracts to fund your next upgrade tier.
- Keep automation steady to prevent crop losses.
